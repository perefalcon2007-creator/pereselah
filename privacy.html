Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Pereselah is a personal daily-devotional app. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can get it deleted — whenever you want, not just now.

📋What we collect

Only what's needed to run your journal — nothing more.

We never collect payment details, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers in Pereselah.

🎯Why we collect it

Strictly to run the app: to know it's you when you sign in, to keep your journal and saved verses with you across every device, and to send the daily reminder if you turn it on. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

🔗Who we share it with

A short list of every outside service Pereselah relies on, and what each one sees.
SupabaseStores your account and journal data, and handles sign-in. Their privacy policy.
Google Sign-InOnly if you choose "Continue with Google" — Google shares your name/email with us, nothing more.
VercelHosts this website. Sees ordinary web traffic logs, not your journal content.
bible-api.comSupplies verse text. Receives only a verse reference (e.g. "John 3:16"), never your identity.

Security rules on our database mean only you can read or write your own entries through the app — not other users, and not us, through the app itself.

🗄️Cookies & local storage

Pereselah doesn't use advertising cookies. It does use your browser's own local storage to remember small things on your device — your theme (light/dark), your reminder settings, and, if you try the app without an account, your guest journal until you sign up. None of this is sent to anyone unless you choose to create an account.

⏳How long we keep it

Your data stays with your account for as long as the account exists. If you delete your account (see below), your journal, saved verses and settings are permanently removed right away — this can't be undone.

🔐Your rights

Delete your account: open the profile menu (top right inside the app) and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your journal, saved verses and settings, and cannot be undone.

You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing us — see Contact below.

🛡️Security

Your connection to Pereselah is encrypted (HTTPS), and database rules ensure your account can only ever read or change its own data. No system is perfectly unbreakable, but we don't store anything beyond what's listed above, which keeps what there is to protect to a minimum.

🧒Children

Pereselah is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

📝Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
