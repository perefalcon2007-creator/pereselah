PERESELAH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Pereselah is a personal daily-devotional app. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your journal reflections — the text you write each day.
  • Verses you save, and which reading plan and reminder settings you choose.

We never collect payment details, and we don't run analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

Only to run the app: to know it's you when you sign in, and to keep your journal and saved verses with you across devices. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Your account and data are stored with Supabase, the database provider behind Pereselah. Security rules on our database mean only you can read or change your own entries — not other users, and not us, through the app itself.

Your rights

Delete your account: open the profile menu (top right) inside the app and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your journal, saved verses and settings, and cannot be undone.

You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your data by emailing us (below).

Children

Pereselah is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
