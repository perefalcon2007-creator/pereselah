QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Your entries are tied to your account and only you can read them.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a quick sign-up with your email. It's what lets your journal and saved verses sync across devices.

What if I miss a day?

No pressure. Your streak simply starts again, and your journal keeps everything you've written.

Can I use it on my phone?

Absolutely. It works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen to open it like an app.
